Adds CMIP7 alongside CMIP6.
Both eras are listed, filtered and served through the same endpoints, and the executions endpoint
takes a new `mip_era` filter.

Results from the two eras are never drawn on the same chart, because the ensembles are not directly
comparable.
Each chart is split on the `mip_id` dimension and labelled with the era it covers.
Observational values carry no era, so they appear on both charts as the shared baseline.
