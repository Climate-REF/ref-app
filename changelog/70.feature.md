Adds CMIP7 alongside CMIP6.
Both eras are listed, filtered and served through the same endpoints.
The executions endpoint takes a new `mip_era` filter.

Results from the two eras are never drawn on the same chart,
because the ensembles are not directly comparable.
Each chart is split on the `mip_id` dimension and labelled with the era it covers.
Observational values carry no era, so they appear on both charts as the shared baseline.
Model values that record no era get their own unlabelled chart rather than being repeated onto both.
